How this works

Live odds come from Kalshi prediction markets (midpoint of bid and ask), refreshed about every 90 seconds by a GitHub Action. Called means Kalshi has settled the market on a media race call. Projected means the market price is 97% or higher, which usually means the race has been called or is about to be. Treat it as market-implied, not an official call.

Pre-election comparison: Kalshi (Nov 2), Decision Desk HQ (Nov 2), On Point Politics (Nov 1), and the final 14-day polling average. For Decision Desk HQ, Lean-Republican races are shown as the challenger's chance (100 minus the favorite's chance).

Headlines come from Google News searches for election results and race calls.

Not affiliated with Kalshi, AP, Decision Desk HQ or any news outlet. Figures may lag live coverage.
